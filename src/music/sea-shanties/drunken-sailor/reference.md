# Drunken Sailor reference

- [Henrik Norbeck song collection](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=www.norbeck.nu%2Fabc%2Fi%2Fhnsong1%2F0103)
- [John Chambers traditional-song transcription](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu%2F~jc%2Fmusic%2Fabc%2Fprogram%2FDrunken_Sailor%2F0000)

The implementation follows the 2/4 verse and chorus and transposes the E-Dorian source down one whole tone to D Dorian, fitting C4–D5.
