# Haul Away Joe reference

- [Dupont Music Circle ABC transcription](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=dmc.lizmilner.com%2Fviewabc.vbhtml%3Ffile%3DHaul+Away+Joe%2F0002)
- [Henrik Norbeck song collection](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=www.norbeck.nu%2Fabc%2Fi%2Fhnsong1%2F0102)

The implementation follows the 2/4 call-and-response melody and transposes the E-minor source down one whole tone so its upper E5 becomes D5.
