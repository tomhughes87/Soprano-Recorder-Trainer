# Early One Morning reference

- [John Chambers ABC collection / Frank Nordberg transcription](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu%2F~jc%2Fmusic%2Fabc%2Fmirror%2FOTIS_Irish_Session%2Fmelnets_big_abc_file%2F03615)
- [IMSLP: Songs of the British Isles](https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/0/00/IMSLP514038-PMLP833171-Hadow_sib_songs.pdf) — “Early one morning” is listed on page 49.

The implementation follows the 2/4 ABC melody in D. Its two source notes below the supported C4–D5 range are octave-folded to A4.
